When a feature needs a number or a rule (chart scale, when to show dots, sizes, limits), find out
what is common practice (web search, chart and design guides, other apps) and pick it yourself;
say the choice and its source in the summary.

**Why:** the user (2026-11-09) deliberately avoids giving exact specs: "you have the whole
internet, find the industry standard; my own opinions are not necessarily the best".

**How to apply:** ask only about the situation and what bothers the user; decide the details,
name them under "decisions taken on your behalf" so they can be corrected.
