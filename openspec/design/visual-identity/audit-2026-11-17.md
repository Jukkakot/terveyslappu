# Terveyslappu: design audit (2026-11-17, code at 44896d3)

Screens: `shots/` next to this file (`<nn>-<screen>-light|dark.png`, 360x780 dp at density 1).
Made-up data only: about 80 kg, a few foods, steps and burned energy.

**Colour caveat.** Robolectric's `dynamicLightColorScheme` gives the AOSP default seed (navy/blue-grey).
On the phone, Material You takes the colours from the wallpaper, so the hues differ. The structure,
the tone roles and the hierarchy match the phone.

## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

The app is consistent and tidy. It uses Material 3 components, has one card language, gets its
spacing roughly right and has dark mode that works. What it lacks is **character and hierarchy**:
- Every surface is the same pale container on the same pale background.
- Every number uses the same default Roboto weight.
- Every chart is one flat primary colour.
- The screens have no single focal point or primary action.

It reads as "a stock Material 3 template filled with data" rather than "a product". The fixes are
mostly tokens and a few components, not a redesign.

## 2. New-user journey (install → first weight → first food)

1. **First open (03).** The screen holds one centred paragraph with an "Anna lupa" button and
   nothing else: no logo, no illustration, no "what you get". The first sentence is technical
   ("tallentaa painon Health Connectiin"). A paid app shows a 2–3 step welcome:
   - a value line ("Punnitse yhdellä napautuksella")
   - why Health Connect is needed (privacy: your data stays on the phone)
   - then the permission.

   *Hobby tell: the permission request IS the onboarding.*
2. **Health Connect check (05).** "Tarkistetaan Health Connectia…" is grey text on an empty page,
   without a spinner. If it takes more than 1 s, it looks frozen.
3. **After permission, no data (04):**
   - The weight card is titled "Historia" (elsewhere "Viimeisin …") and says "Punnitse ⋮-valikosta
     tai widgetistä". It points at a menu instead of offering a button.
   - Food and steps cards show "Ruoka: anna lupa" / "Askeleet: anna lupa" in **headline size**,
     wrapped over two lines. It looks like content, not an action. There is no button shape, no
     icon and no reason given.

   *The first thing a new user sees is three cards that say "not yet".*
4. **First weight.**
   - The daily action (Punnitse) lives only in the ⋮ menu, the widget, the tile or the shortcut.
     The front page has no FAB or button. A new user has to discover the menu.
   - The weigh-in dialog (31–36) is clean, but the saved state (33) is anticlimactic: "Tallennettu"
     in small primary text, with no check mark, no change and no context.
5. **First food.** Ruoka has a good full-width "Lisää annos" button at the bottom (20, 21). The
   empty state (21) is a plain sentence. The add sheet (22b) is functional. The suggestion chips
   stack one per line and waste room. Each row carries a loud red bin.
6. **Discoverability.**
   - Edit mode is reached only by long-press, explained in Ohje.
   - Ohje (50) is a wall of prose cards that refer to "⋮-valikon …" by glyph.
   - Nobody reads it. Contextual hints would do the job.

## 4. Findings by screen

Effort S < half a day, M 1–2 days, L more. Impact is how much nicer it feels to a new or daily user.

**Front page (01, 02, 04)**
1. **No primary action on the front page.** Punnitse and Lisää annos live in ⋮. *Fix:* a FAB
   (Punnitse), or an extended FAB with a small "+ annos" companion, bottom right. Or put an inline
   "Punnitse" tonal button in the weight card when today has no weight. **S, high.**
2. **Card titles truncate:** "Syöty tänä…", "Askeleet tä…" at half width. *Fix:* use "Syöty" and
   "Askeleet" with "tänään" under the number, or drop "tänään" (the number already says today).
   **S, med.**
3. **The period chip is the visual heaviest element** (dark filled pill, 01). *Fix:* an
   outlined/assist chip or a plain text button "1 vk ▾" in labelLarge, or move the period to card
   settings. **S, med.**
4. **Average text wraps** "ka 944 / kcal/pv" (01). *Fix:* one line ("Ø 944 kcal/pv" or

7. **Permission cards (04)** show "Ruoka: anna lupa" as a headline. *Fix:* an icon, one line of

## 5. Glossary and wording violations

Checked against product.md "Terms":
- **Average has four names:** "Keskiarvo" (Paino tile), "Keskimäärin" (Syöty/Askeleet tiles),
  "keskim." (long card text, Kulutus "Aktiivinen keskim."), "ka" (cards), plus legend
  "keskiarvo". *Pick one:* "keskiarvo" in tiles and legends, "ka" only where space forces it
  (or "keskim." everywhere).
- **Manual entry:** Ohje says "Syötä paino käsin"; the menu says "Lisää paino käsin".
- **The add sheet has three names:** "Lisää annos -ikkuna", "lisäysikkuna" (Ohje) and the sheet.
  Ohje also uses "Ruoka-näkymä" where the glossary term is "Ruoka".
- **Weight card title:** "Viimeisin …" / "Historia" (empty) / "Paino" (edit mode).
- **Day counts:** "Askelpäiviä" (Askeleet) vs "Päiviä" (Syöty, Kulutus) for the same concept.
- **Steps setting:** the glossary says "Mistä askeleet luetaan"; the Asetukset row is "Askeleet".
  Fine as a list title if the page heading uses the glossary term; check.
- **"anna lupa":** capitalised in buttons, lower-case inside card headlines ("Ruoka: anna lupa");
  pick one pattern.
- **Date formats:** "Su 15.11." vs "Sunnuntaina 15.11." (not glossary, but the same concept named
  two ways).

## 6. Modern look: three directions

Benchmark: today's big-company health apps (Google Fit / Fitbit's 2024+ refresh, Samsung Health,
Apple Health, Withings, Oura). Common traits:
- one confident accent per data type
- big numerals in a heavier or rounded display face
- tinted cards instead of grey ones
- rounded, gradient-filled charts with today highlighted
- 24–28 dp shapes
- expressive motion on save

Material 3 Expressive (2025) formalises much of this:
- shape morphing and larger corner families
- stronger tonal contrast
- emphasised type styles (heavier display/headline)
- springy motion

None of the directions copies a look. Each names the pattern it borrows.

### A. Calm grey + one strong accent ("Quiet instrument")  **Recommended**

Keeps the grey the user likes and adds one signature colour, so the app gets an identity without
getting louder.
- **Colour roles:**
  - Neutral surfaces stay (dynamic or fixed neutral).
  - One fixed brand accent: a saturated teal-green from the icon (`#2E7D6B` family), generated as
    a full M3 tonal palette (material-color-utilities), used for primary actions, the hero number
    and today's chart mark.
  - Per-quantity accents as fixed secondary hues, used only in charts and card icons: paino
    accent, Syöty warm amber, Askeleet blue, Kulutus coral.
  - Option in Ulkoasu: "Puhelimen värit" (dynamic) vs "Terveyslappu" (brand). Default: brand.
- **Cards:**
  - Background `surface`, cards `surfaceContainerLow` with no outline, 24 dp corners.
  - Each card's icon sits in a small round tinted tile of its quantity colour.
  - The hero card (paino) gets a faint accent tint (`primaryContainer` at ~30 %).
- **Type:** keep Roboto. Big numerals in `displayMedium` with **SemiBold / tabular figures**
  (Roboto Flex if available); units in `titleMedium` muted. Labels: one style (labelLarge) for
  card titles, one (bodySmall) for meta.
- **Shapes:** 24 dp cards, 16 dp tiles, full-round chips. Rows lose dividers and use 8 dp gaps.
- **Charts:**
  - Weight: a 2.5 dp line with a soft gradient area fill to transparent.
  - Bars: 4 dp rounded tops, today in full accent, past days at 60 % tone.
  - Average: a dashed line labelled at the right edge ("ka 944").
  - No legend on single-series charts; gridlines at 8 % opacity.
- **Motion:** the number counts up on load; a check-mark morph on save; cards spring on edit-mode
  entry.
- **Borrows from:** Withings / Oura (neutral canvas, one accent per metric, big numerals).

### B. Colour-coded tiles ("Dashboard")

Bolder: each quantity owns a colour, and its card is filled with that colour's container tone.
- **Colour roles:**
  - Paino primaryContainer, Syöty amber container, Askeleet blue container, Kulutus coral
    container.
  - Text in the matching on-container colour.
  - App chrome stays neutral.
- **Cards:** filled tinted cards with no outline, 28 dp corners. The front page becomes a coloured
  mosaic; charts drawn in the on-container colour.
- **Type:** an expressive display weight (Bold), large today values, tiny labels.
- **Charts:** inside cards, mono-colour sparkbars in the card's own colour. In Historia, the tab's
  colour drives the whole page (the indicator, the chips and the chart).
- **Risk:** loud. The weight colour must stay neutral (no "good/bad"; product.md: no red or green
  for gains).
- **Borrows from:** Samsung Health / Google Fit tiles (colour per metric, tile grid).

### C. Dark-first editorial ("Night stand")

The most distinctive: a deep blue-grey or graphite default (light theme kept), white numerals,
neon-ish accent lines.
- **Colour:** custom dark scheme (surface `#121418`, containers stepped by +4 % lightness); one
  luminous accent (mint or lime) for lines and today; per-metric hues desaturated.
- **Cards:** borderless, separated by spacing and a hairline at most; big type carries the
  hierarchy.
- **Type:** a rounded or condensed display face for numerals (e.g. Google Sans Flex / Inter
  Display via downloadable fonts), set large; small caps-free labels.
- **Charts:** glowing line (accent + 20 % blur halo), area gradient, no gridlines, axis labels only
  at the ends.
- **Risk:** the morning weigh-in happens in bright bathrooms, so the light theme still matters.
  More custom work, and it drifts from Material You.
- **Borrows from:** Oura / Whoop (dark canvas, luminous single accent).

**Why A:** it keeps the user's taste (calm grey), fixes the "no identity" and "flat hierarchy"
problems with tokens rather than new screens, stays inside Material 3 (no fight with components),
and is the cheapest. Most of it is one theme file, a chart-style object and a handful of component
tweaks.

## 7. Motivation & psychology

Evidence is summarised with sources in `openspec/context/knowledge.md` → "Motivation and feedback"
(committed on this branch). The product.md decisions that apply:
- BR5: no streaks, red numbers or judgement.
- "no gamification, no week-to-week comparison features for now".
- "show facts, never smoothed values".
- no step goal.
- goal weight and forecast are in the backlog.

Ideas that would reopen one of these are marked **[reopens: …]**.

1. **Long-term view: "Matka" card / Historia header.** **Recommended.**
   - *What the user sees:* a small "Since start" line: "3 kk sitten 83,4 → nyt 80,2 kg", and on
     Historia "Kaikki" a single far-zoomed line from the first weight. Optionally "Viime kuukausi
     vs. 3 kk sitten" as monthly averages (facts, not smoothing).
   - *Evidence:* visible progress and the progress principle; daily noise of ±1 kg hides the
     trend, while monthly means do not.
   - *Risk:* if the trend is up, this card turns into bad news, so keep neutral colours and allow
     hiding it.
   - *Effort and impact:* S–M, high.
   - [reopens: none if raw monthly averages; a smoothed line reopens "never smoothed"].
2. **Monthly averages instead of single readings for change.**
   - *What the user sees:* the Historia list grouped by month (already) shows each month's
     average, with a neutral arrow against the previous month.
   - *Evidence:* trend beats single readings; bad news weighs 2–3x, and a monthly mean rarely
     flips with daily noise.
   - *Effort and impact:* S, med.
   - [reopens: "no week-to-week comparison" only loosely, since this is month-to-month].
3. **Positive reinforcement after the act, not the result.**
   - *What the user sees:* the weigh-in "Tallennettu" state with an animated check and a fact
     that is always true and kind, such as "12. punnitus tässä kuussa" or "Punnitus tallennettu
     Health Connectiin".
   - *Evidence:* logging is the active ingredient; praise the behaviour, not the number.
     Tailored messages added no weight loss in SMARTER, so keep it to one line, no nagging.
   - *Risk:* low.
   - *Effort and impact:* S, med–high.
4. **Consistency without streaks: "punnituspäivät" dots.**
   - *What the user sees:* a 4-week row of 28 small dots (filled = weighed) on the weight card or
     in Historia, with no counter and nothing that resets to zero.
   - *Evidence:* one missed day does not hurt habit formation; reset-to-zero streaks invite the
     "what-the-hell" quit; weighing less goes with regain.
   - *Risk:* it can still feel like a streak; keep it quiet and never colour gaps red.
   - *Effort and impact:* S, med.
   - [reopens: BR5 "no streaks" partly; it is a calendar, not a streak, but ask].
5. **Fresh-start nudges.**
   - *What the user sees:* on the 1st of a month or a Monday, a dismissible line: "Uusi kuukausi —
     lokakuun keskiarvo 81,0 kg." For food: "Uusi viikko: kirjaa muutama päivä, niin näet
     tavallisen viikon." This fits BR1 (log for a learning period).
   - *Evidence:* fresh-start effect (Dai, Milkman, Riis 2014).
   - *Risk:* nagging; once per landmark, dismissible.
   - *Effort and impact:* S, med.
5b. **Food logging in short learning bursts.**
   - *What the user sees:* "Oppimisjakso: 7 päivää" in Ruoka, a gentle start with an end date and
     a summary at the end ("Tavallinen päiväsi: aamupala ~350, lounas ~600 …").
   - *Evidence:* logging adherence drops after 3–5 weeks for most people; a bounded period with a
     payoff fits BR1 exactly.
   - *Effort and impact:* M, high.
6. **Colour without shame.**
   - Keep gains and losses in neutral palette tones (already decided). Use the brand accent for
     *actions and today*, never for good or bad.
   - Show BMI tones only in Historia, not on the front page; product.md already shows them only as
     tones. Consider dropping red from the BMI bands too: amber is enough.
   - *Evidence:* "red lowers performance" did not replicate, but red's meaning (error, failure) is
     clear, and gains already feel 2–3x worse.
   - *Effort and impact:* S, med.
7. **Small moving goals (optional).**
   - *What the user sees:* an opt-in "Seuraava välietappi: 79,5 kg" 0.5–1 kg below the current
     monthly average, which moves as they progress, with no deadline.
   - *Evidence:* Uetake and Yang: small, shifting goals beat one far goal.
   - *Risk:* obsession or disappointment.
   - *Effort and impact:* M, med.
   - [reopens: goal weight is in the backlog].
8. **Morning-ritual framing.**
   - *What the user sees:* the widget or tile result shows the weight and the 7-day range
     ("viikon vaihteluväli 79,8–80,6"), so a +0.4 kg day sits visibly inside normal noise.
   - *Evidence:* daily noise of 0.5–2 kg is mostly water; knowing the range cuts alarm.
   - *Effort and impact:* S, high.
   - [reopens: none; a range is a fact].
9. **Avoid scale avoidance.**
   - *What the user sees:* after a gap of 5+ days, the first weigh-in says "Tervetuloa takaisin"
     and nothing about the change; the change shows only against the previous monthly average.
   - *Evidence:* people who gain tend to stop weighing (scale-avoidance hypothesis); a gentle
     return lowers the cost of coming back.
   - *Effort and impact:* S, med.

Not recommended:
- leaderboards or social features
- badges for kg lost
- calorie budgets (product.md BR2)
- step goals (decided against)
- push reminders by default (weighing is already one tap; at most an opt-in reminder)

## 9. Functional gaps a paid app would have (brief)

- Onboarding and a "what's new" note after updates. The version jumps silently through Obtainium.
- A Tietoa page: privacy statement, licences (MPAndroidChart, POI, Reorderable, Open Food Facts
  ODbL), contact.
- An opt-in gentle reminder (morning weigh-in, food in a learning period).
- A glanceable widget with the latest weight and a sparkline (2x1); Wear OS tile or complication.
- Goal weight and forecast (backlog).
- Body measurements (waist) and photos; notes on a day ("sick", "travel").
